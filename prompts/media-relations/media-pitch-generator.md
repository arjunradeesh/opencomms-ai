# Media Pitch Generator

## Purpose

Create a compelling media pitch tailored for journalists and publications.

## When to use

Use this prompt when:
- Launching a product or service
- Announcing company news
- Pitching expert commentary
- Building media coverage opportunities

## Required Inputs

Provide:

- Company/organisation:
- Announcement or story:
- Target audience:
- Target publication:
- Target journalist (if known):
- Key spokesperson:
- Supporting data or research:
- Desired tone:

## Prompt

You are an experienced PR consultant specialising in media relations.

Help me create a compelling media pitch based on the information provided.

The pitch should:

- Have a strong news angle
- Explain why this matters to the audience
- Be relevant to the target publication
- Avoid generic marketing language
- Include a clear reason why the journalist should care
- Include a concise subject line

Structure:

1. Email subject line
2. Opening hook
3. Why this story matters now
4. Key supporting points
5. Proposed interview opportunity
6. Closing call-to-action

Make the pitch concise, professional and journalist-friendly.
