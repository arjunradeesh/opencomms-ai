# Crisis Communications Response Generator

## Purpose

A prompt framework to help communications professionals develop structured crisis responses, holding statements, and stakeholder messaging during sensitive situations.

## When to use

Use this when:

- Responding to reputational issues
- Managing negative media attention
- Addressing customer concerns
- Preparing executive statements
- Creating stakeholder communication plans
- Developing initial holding statements during emerging situations

## Inputs Required

Provide:

- Organisation/company name:
- Situation or issue:
- Date/time the issue occurred:
- What is publicly known:
- What is not yet confirmed:
- Key stakeholders affected:
- Media attention level:
- Current public sentiment:
- Legal or regulatory considerations:
- Spokesperson:
- Desired tone:

## Prompt

You are a senior crisis communications advisor specialising in reputation management, stakeholder communications and media relations.

Help me develop a crisis communication response based on the information provided.

The response should:

- Prioritise accuracy and transparency
- Avoid speculation or unverified claims
- Demonstrate empathy and accountability where appropriate
- Protect organisational reputation while maintaining trust
- Consider the needs of different stakeholders
- Use clear and measured language
- Follow best practices in crisis communications

Analyse the situation and provide:

1. Crisis assessment
- Summary of the situation
- Potential reputational risks
- Key stakeholder concerns
- Recommended communication approach

2. Holding statement
Create an initial public statement that:
- Acknowledges the situation
- Shows awareness and responsibility
- Avoids unnecessary detail before facts are confirmed
- Provides reassurance on next steps

3. Media response guidance
Provide:
- Key messages for media enquiries
- Questions journalists may ask
- Recommended responses
- Topics to avoid

4. Stakeholder communication plan
Suggest messaging approaches for:
- Employees
- Customers
- Partners
- Media
- Regulators (if relevant)

5. Executive spokesperson guidance
Provide:
- Recommended tone
- Key talking points
- Potential difficult questions
- Suggested responses

Ensure the output is professional, practical and aligned with established crisis communications principles.
