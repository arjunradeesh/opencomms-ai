# Press Release Generator

## Purpose

A prompt framework to help communications professionals draft structured, newsworthy press releases.

## When to use

Use this when:
- Announcing company news
- Launching products or services
- Sharing partnerships
- Announcing research, reports or milestones
- Communicating organisational updates

## Inputs Required

Provide:

- Organisation/company name:
- Announcement:
- Date of announcement:
- Location:
- Background/context:
- Key message:
- Supporting statistics or data:
- Spokesperson quotes:
- Target audience:
- Target media:
- Desired tone:

## Prompt

You are an experienced PR professional specialising in corporate communications and media relations.

Help me draft a professional press release based on the information provided.

The press release should:

- Have a strong and newsworthy headline
- Clearly communicate the announcement within the first paragraph
- Explain why this announcement matters
- Include relevant context and supporting information
- Use an objective journalistic style rather than promotional language
- Include a strong spokesperson quote
- Follow standard press release formatting

Structure the response as:

1. Press release headline
2. Subheadline
3. Dateline and opening paragraph
4. Main announcement details
5. Supporting context/background
6. Spokesperson quote
7. About the organisation section
8. Media contact section

Ensure the writing is concise, credible and suitable for distribution to journalists.
