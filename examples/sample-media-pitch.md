# Sample Media Pitch Example

## Purpose

This example demonstrates how an AI-assisted media pitch workflow can help PR professionals develop journalist outreach.

---

# Scenario

## Organisation

OpenComms AI

## Announcement

OpenComms AI launches an open-source toolkit helping communications professionals use artificial intelligence for PR workflows.

## Target Audience

PR professionals, communications teams, marketers, journalists, and content creators.

## Target Media

Marketing, technology, business, and communications publications.

---

# Input Prompt

You are an experienced PR consultant specialising in media relations.

Create a journalist-friendly media pitch based on the following announcement:

OpenComms AI has launched an open-source toolkit designed to help communications professionals integrate artificial intelligence into everyday PR workflows.

The toolkit provides practical AI prompts and workflows covering media relations, press releases, crisis communications, and content creation.

The pitch should:
- Highlight why this matters now
- Explain the relevance to communications professionals
- Avoid promotional language
- Create a clear reason for journalists to engage

Structure:
1. Subject line
2. Opening hook
3. Story angle
4. Supporting points
5. Interview opportunity
6. Closing call-to-action

---

# Example Output

## Subject:

How AI is reshaping the future of PR: New open-source toolkit helps communicators work smarter

## Opening Hook:

As artificial intelligence continues to transform how organisations communicate, PR professionals are looking for practical ways to integrate AI into their everyday workflows. OpenComms AI introduces an open-source toolkit designed specifically for communications teams, helping them apply AI to media relations, content development, and strategic storytelling.

## Story Angle:

While many AI resources focus on technical teams and developers, communications professionals often lack practical frameworks designed around their specific workflows. OpenComms AI aims to bridge this gap by providing accessible resources for PR teams, marketers, and journalists.

## Key Supporting Points:

- Open-source resource designed specifically for communications professionals
- Covers practical PR workflows including media relations and crisis communications
- Helps teams explore responsible AI adoption
- Encourages collaboration and knowledge sharing within the communications community

## Interview Opportunity:

The project creator is available to discuss:
- The changing role of AI in communications
- How PR professionals can adopt AI responsibly
- The future of AI-assisted storytelling

## Closing:

Would you be interested in exploring how AI is changing the way communications professionals approach their work?

---

# Learning

This example demonstrates how AI can support PR professionals while maintaining human judgement, strategic thinking, and editorial review.
