# Podcast Production Workflow

## Purpose

A structured AI-assisted workflow for planning, producing, publishing, and repurposing podcast episodes.

The workflow is designed to help podcast hosts, producers, communications professionals, and content teams use AI throughout the podcast production lifecycle while keeping human editorial judgement at the centre.

## When to use

Use this workflow when:

- Developing a new podcast episode
- Preparing for an interview
- Researching a guest
- Structuring an episode
- Creating show notes
- Developing promotional content
- Repurposing an episode across multiple channels
- Reviewing episode performance

## Step 1: Define the Episode Objective

Identify:

- Podcast name:
- Episode number:
- Episode topic:
- Guest:
- Target audience:
- Primary objective:
- Key takeaway:
- Desired tone:
- Distribution channels:

Possible objectives include:

- Educating the audience
- Telling a compelling story
- Exploring an industry topic
- Building thought leadership
- Highlighting a guest's expertise
- Generating social content
- Supporting a broader communications campaign

---

## Step 2: Research the Guest and Topic

Use AI to organise research into:

### Guest Background

- Professional experience
- Areas of expertise
- Previous roles
- Relevant achievements
- Publicly available perspectives

### Topic Research

- Current developments
- Relevant trends
- Supporting data
- Industry context
- Potential areas of disagreement or debate

### Editorial Opportunities

Identify:

- Interesting stories
- Contrarian perspectives
- Unusual experiences
- Areas where the guest can provide unique insight

Always verify research independently before recording or publishing.

---

## Step 3: Develop the Episode Concept

Create a clear episode concept that defines:

- Central theme
- Audience value
- Main narrative
- Key questions
- Desired takeaway

The concept should be specific enough to guide the conversation without making the episode feel overly scripted.

---

## Step 4: Prepare the Interview

Develop questions across three levels:

### Opening Questions

Designed to establish context and introduce the guest naturally.

### Core Questions

Designed to explore the central topic in depth.

### Follow-up Questions

Designed to respond to interesting answers and encourage deeper discussion.

Also prepare:

- Potential follow-up questions
- Sensitive topics requiring careful handling
- Clarification questions
- Closing questions

Avoid creating a rigid script. The host should be able to follow the conversation naturally.

---

## Step 5: Build the Episode Structure

Create an episode structure such as:

### Introduction

- Podcast introduction
- Guest introduction
- Episode premise

### Context

Establish the topic and why it matters.

### Main Conversation

Explore the central themes through questions and follow-ups.

### Personal Perspective

Explore the guest's experiences, lessons, challenges, and opinions.

### Key Takeaways

Summarise the strongest insights.

### Closing

- Final question
- Guest's recommended resources or next steps
- Where listeners can find the guest
- Podcast sign-off

---

## Step 6: Recording Preparation

Before recording, prepare:

- Recording checklist
- Guest briefing
- Technical requirements
- Episode structure
- Key questions
- Backup questions
- Intro and outro

AI can help create checklists and preparation documents, but technical setup and recording quality should be verified by the production team.

---

## Step 7: Editing & Editorial Review

Use AI-assisted analysis where appropriate to identify:

- Repetitive sections
- Long pauses
- Unclear statements
- Potentially confusing sections
- Strong quotes
- Strong narrative moments
- Potential short-form clips

Human editorial review should determine what is ultimately removed, retained, or published.

Do not rely on AI alone to determine factual accuracy, context, tone, or editorial suitability.

---

## Step 8: Create Show Notes

Generate:

- Episode title options
- Episode description
- Short summary
- Key discussion points
- Guest biography
- Relevant links
- Timestamps
- Keywords

The final copy should be edited for accuracy and consistency with the podcast's editorial voice.

---

## Step 9: Repurpose the Episode

Transform the episode into:

### Social Content

- LinkedIn posts
- Instagram captions
- X posts
- Short-form video captions

### Editorial Content

- Blog articles
- Newsletter content
- Executive insights
- Quote cards
- Industry commentary

### Short-Form Video

Identify potential clips based on:

- Strong insights
- Surprising statements
- Emotional moments
- Practical advice
- Debate-worthy opinions
- Memorable quotes

Each piece should be adapted to the platform rather than simply copied across channels.

---

## Step 10: Distribution

Prepare:

- Episode title
- Description
- Show notes
- Metadata
- Thumbnail or artwork requirements
- Social launch copy
- Guest promotional assets
- Publishing schedule

Create a distribution checklist to ensure all platforms and promotional channels are covered.

---

## Step 11: Performance Analysis

After publication, review:

- Downloads or plays
- Average listening duration
- Audience retention
- Completion rate
- Engagement
- Social performance
- Website traffic
- Audience feedback

Use the results to identify:

- Topics that resonated
- Questions that generated engagement
- Strong-performing content formats
- Opportunities for future episodes

---

## Best Practices

- Use AI to accelerate production, not replace editorial judgement.
- Verify all factual claims and research.
- Maintain the authentic voice of the host and guest.
- Avoid making AI-generated content sound overly polished or generic.
- Obtain appropriate consent before publishing or repurposing guest material.
- Review sensitive or potentially defamatory content carefully.
- Adapt content to each platform rather than publishing identical material everywhere.
- Keep the audience's needs at the centre of the production process.
