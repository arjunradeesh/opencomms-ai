# Executive Thought Leadership Workflow

## Purpose

A structured AI-assisted workflow to help communications professionals develop credible, insight-led thought leadership for executives and subject-matter experts.

## When to use

Use this workflow when:

- Developing an executive's thought leadership platform
- Turning expertise into media commentary
- Creating LinkedIn content for senior leaders
- Developing bylined articles
- Preparing conference or panel talking points
- Building a long-term executive visibility strategy

## Step 1: Define the Executive Profile

Provide:

- Executive name:
- Job title:
- Organisation:
- Industry:
- Areas of expertise:
- Professional background:
- Key achievements:
- Personal perspective or opinions:
- Existing public profile:

Use AI to identify the executive's strongest areas of authority and credibility.

---

## Step 2: Define the Audience and Objective

Identify:

- Primary audience
- Secondary audiences
- Business objectives
- Communication objectives
- Desired perception
- Desired audience action

Possible objectives include:

- Building executive visibility
- Establishing industry authority
- Supporting corporate reputation
- Generating media opportunities
- Building stakeholder trust
- Supporting business development

---

## Step 3: Map Areas of Expertise

Organise the executive's expertise into three categories:

### Core Expertise

Topics where the executive has established authority and experience.

### Emerging Expertise

Topics where the executive has relevant knowledge or a developing perspective.

### Market Perspective

Broader industry trends where the executive can provide informed commentary.

Avoid positioning the executive as an expert in areas where they lack credible experience.

---

## Step 4: Identify Thought Leadership Territories

Develop 3–5 long-term thought leadership territories.

For each territory, identify:

- Why the topic matters
- Why the executive is credible to discuss it
- The audience affected
- Relevant industry trends
- Potential points of view
- Potential media opportunities

Prioritise territories that allow the executive to contribute a distinctive perspective rather than simply repeat commonly accepted industry opinions.

---

## Step 5: Develop Content Angles

For each thought leadership territory, generate potential angles for:

- LinkedIn posts
- Bylined articles
- Media commentary
- Interviews
- Conference panels
- Podcasts
- Industry reports
- Opinion pieces

Each angle should include:

- Working headline
- Core argument
- Why it matters now
- Supporting evidence required
- Executive point of view
- Target audience
- Recommended format

---

## Step 6: Develop the Content

Use AI to create the selected content while preserving the executive's authentic voice.

The content should:

- Lead with an insight rather than a company promotion
- Present a clear point of view
- Include relevant evidence or examples
- Avoid generic AI-generated language
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Reflect the executive's actual expertise
- Provide practical value to the audience

AI should support the executive's thinking rather than replace it.

---

## Step 7: Adapt for Different Channels

Adapt the core idea for:

### LinkedIn

Create a concise, opinion-led post designed to encourage professional discussion.

### Media Commentary

Create a concise expert perspective suitable for journalists seeking commentary.

### Bylined Article

Develop a structured argument supported by evidence, examples, and expert insight.

### Podcast or Interview

Create talking points, key messages, and potential questions.

### Conference Panel

Create concise talking points and memorable perspectives that can be delivered naturally.

---

## Step 8: Review and Refine

Before publishing, assess the content against:

### Authority

Does the executive have genuine expertise to discuss this topic?

### Originality

Does the content offer a distinctive perspective?

### Relevance

Does it address an issue that matters to the intended audience?

### Evidence

Are claims supported by reliable data, research, or experience?

### Authenticity

Does it sound like the executive rather than an AI-generated corporate statement?

### Clarity

Can the central argument be understood quickly?

### Risk

Could any statement create reputational, legal, regulatory, or stakeholder concerns?

---

## Step 9: Build an Ongoing Thought Leadership Plan

Create a rolling content plan that balances:

- Evergreen expertise
- Industry trends
- Newsjacking opportunities
- Original perspectives
- Data and research
- Personal professional experiences
- Relevant company developments

Aim to build a recognisable point of view over time rather than treating each piece of content as a standalone activity.

---

## Best Practices

- Start with the executive's genuine expertise.
- Develop a clear and defensible point of view.
- Use AI to explore ideas, not manufacture expertise.
- Verify statistics, research, and factual claims.
- Preserve the executive's authentic voice.
- Avoid excessive corporate language.
- Separate personal opinion from organisational positions where necessary.
- Review content for legal, regulatory, and reputational risks.
- Treat thought leadership as a long-term reputation-building strategy.
