# PR Campaign Planning Workflow

## Purpose

A structured AI-assisted workflow to help communications professionals plan, develop, and execute PR campaigns.

## When to use

Use this workflow when:

- Launching a product or service
- Creating a thought leadership campaign
- Building media awareness
- Planning announcements
- Developing integrated communication strategies

## Workflow Overview

Campaign Objective

↓

Audience Identification

↓

Key Message Development

↓

Story Angle Creation

↓

Media Targeting

↓

Content Development

↓

Media Outreach

↓

Measurement & Reporting


## AI Workflow

### Step 1: Define the Campaign Objective

Use AI to clarify:

- Business objectives
- Communication goals
- Target audiences
- Desired outcomes
- Success metrics


### Step 2: Develop Key Messaging

Use AI to create:

- Core message
- Supporting messages
- Audience-specific messaging
- Proof points
- Spokesperson positioning


### Step 3: Develop Story Angles

Use AI to identify:

- Newsworthy angles
- Audience relevance
- Industry trends
- Media opportunities


### Step 4: Build Media Strategy

Use AI to support:

- Target publication research
- Journalist identification
- Pitch development
- Outreach planning


### Step 5: Create Communication Assets

Generate:

- Press releases
- Media pitches
- Executive talking points
- Social media content
- Interview preparation materials


### Step 6: Measure Impact

Evaluate:

- Media coverage
- Audience engagement
- Message adoption
- Campaign performance


## Best Practices

- Always verify AI-generated information
- Apply human judgement and communication expertise
- Ensure messaging aligns with organisational values
- Review outputs for accuracy, tone, and audience relevance
